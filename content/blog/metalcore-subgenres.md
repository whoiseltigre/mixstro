Breakdowns, riffs and raw emotion: metalcore has splintered into a lot of branches over the years, each with its own sound. Here are the major ones, with the bands that defined them and a track to start with.

## Classic metalcore

Classic metalcore emerged in the ’90s, blending hardcore punk’s raw power with metal’s technicality. It’s known for its intense breakdowns, aggressive vocals, and high-energy riffs.

**Bands to start with:**

- **Converge** – [Concubine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SKmOLanlqoY): Known for chaotic energy, Converge is a foundational band, best known for their iconic album *Jane Doe*.
- **Earth Crisis** – [Firestorm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s2dTHdJLVzA): Mixing social activism with their music, Earth Crisis brought a serious edge to metalcore and became legends of the early scene.
- **Hatebreed** – [I Will Be Heard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HB3tmC2GSDE): With motivational, hard-hitting lyrics, Hatebreed became a go-to name in classic metalcore.
- **Integrity** – [Systems Overload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Kb61dCJrQw): Integrity’s dark themes and intense sound helped define early metalcore’s gritty aesthetic.
- **Shai Hulud** – [A Profound Hatred of Man](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Id7hnQX6Qf4): Known for introspective lyrics, Shai Hulud is a respected name in early metalcore.

**The vibe:** Basement shows, raw power, and unfiltered intensity.

## Melodic metalcore

Melodic metalcore is the most accessible branch, blending aggressive riffs with catchy choruses and clean vocals. It hit its peak in the early 2000s, appealing to fans of heavy music who enjoy a strong melody.

**Bands to start with:**

- **Killswitch Engage** – [My Last Serenade](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPW9AbRMwFU): Known for balancing harsh screams with clean vocals, Killswitch Engage became leaders of the melodic sound.
- **As I Lay Dying** – [The Sound of Truth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=46Dohh51nVw): Combining intricate guitar work with powerful lyrics, As I Lay Dying mastered melodic metalcore’s dynamic sound.
- **Bullet for My Valentine** – [Tears Don’t Fall](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sTQ0QdkN3Q): This band brought emotional lyrics and big hooks, making them a melodic favorite.
- **Parkway Drive** – [Carrion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5BvQj8tqIEw): Known for crowd-pleasing breakdowns and melodic riffs, Parkway Drive are essential listening.
- **August Burns Red** – [White Washed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0LIyOJko7c): This band’s technical riffs and powerful vocals add a unique edge to melodic metalcore.

**The vibe:** Big anthems, high energy, and riffs that stick.

## Deathcore

Deathcore blends death metal’s brutality with metalcore’s breakdowns. With guttural vocals and earth-shattering riffs, it’s the heaviest branch of the family.

**Bands to start with:**

- **Suicide Silence** – [Unanswered](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KJbzgU3EsE0): Known for punishing breakdowns, Suicide Silence brought deathcore into the metalcore mainstream.
- **Whitechapel** – [This Is Exile](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQaFHvhb0cE): Whitechapel’s relentless sound makes them a must-know deathcore band.
- **Thy Art Is Murder** – [Reign of Darkness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rpQqCydPyk8): Their aggressive style and political lyrics have made them a leading deathcore act.
- **Carnifex** – [Hell Chose Me](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E5EoFJPt3DU): Known for their dark atmosphere, Carnifex pushes deathcore’s sound to its heaviest limits.
- **Chelsea Grin** – [Recreant](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-gR-Po0Ms1Y): Combining heavy breakdowns with intense vocals, Chelsea Grin is a key name in deathcore.

**The vibe:** Dark themes, brutal riffs, and unrestrained heaviness.

## Progressive metalcore

Progressive metalcore pushes musical boundaries, using technical complexity, unusual time signatures, and genre-blending elements. It’s perfect if you like your heavy music technical and a little strange.

**Bands to start with:**

- **Between the Buried and Me** – [Selkies: The Endless Obsession](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3G1hzCoqF0Y): Known for genre-bending sounds, BTBAM is a cornerstone of progressive metalcore.
- **Periphery** – [Scarlet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MfOnq-zXXBw): This band combines technical proficiency with complex rhythms, making them giants of the style.
- **The Contortionist** – [Language I: Intuition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zg2077AYfhg): Their ethereal sound and intricate compositions make The Contortionist stand out in progressive metalcore.
- **Architects** – [Doomsday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvWbcK3YQ_o): Architects combine intricate lyrics and complex instrumentals, carving out their own niche.
- **Northlane** – [Quantum Flux](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UM15xlDUfN4): Mixing atmospheric elements with heavy riffs, Northlane adds a futuristic touch to progressive metalcore.

**The vibe:** Experimental sounds, technical riffs, and intricate, layered compositions.
