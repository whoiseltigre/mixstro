The best music production tools now do jobs that used to take an engineer and a whole afternoon. AI can pull a vocal out of a finished mix. A groovebox the size of a laptop can replace the laptop. None of it writes the song for you, but it clears a lot of busywork out of the way.

Here's what's worth knowing about, whether you produce your own tracks or record your band's demos at home.

## Music production software

### Stem separation

Stem separation splits a finished recording into vocals, drums, bass and everything else. [AudioShake](https://www.audioshake.ai/) is one of the best-known dedicated tools, and stem splitting is now built into major DAWs like Logic Pro and FL Studio. Check what you already own before you pay for anything.

It's useful in more ways than remixing. You can solo the bass line of a song you're learning, or rescue an old live recording of your band whose multitracks are long gone.

### Chord detection

[Chord AI](https://chordai.net/) listens to a song and shows the chords as it plays. When a bandmate sends you a song to learn before Thursday's rehearsal, it saves you a lot of rewinding. It's also a quick way to check what you just played while writing.

## Production hardware

### Native Instruments Maschine+

[Maschine+](https://www.native-instruments.com/) is a standalone groovebox: pads, sounds and a sequencer, no computer needed. It's built for sketching beats on the couch and playing live, and it's easier to stay creative when you're not staring at a screen.

### ROLI Seaboard RISE 2

The [Seaboard RISE 2](https://roli.com/) swaps piano keys for a soft, continuous surface. You slide, press and wiggle to bend pitch, swell volume and shape tone, much like a string player or singer would. It brings real expression to synth parts.

## Online tools and services

### LANDR

[LANDR](https://www.landr.com/) started with AI mastering and grew into a release toolkit with mastering, distribution, samples and plugins. Its mastering is a quick way to get a demo loud and polished before you're ready to pay a mastering engineer.

### Splice

[Splice](https://splice.com/) is a huge library of royalty-free samples and loops, with search that helps you find sounds that fit your track. You'll spend less time digging for the right snare and more time finishing songs.
