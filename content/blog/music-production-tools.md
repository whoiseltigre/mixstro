Home production keeps getting more powerful. AI now handles jobs that used to take an engineer and an afternoon, and hardware keeps getting better at letting you make music away from the laptop. Here are the tools worth knowing about, whether you produce your own songs or record your band's demos.

## Software

### Stem separation

Stem separation splits a finished recording into vocals, drums, bass and other parts. It's great for remixing, sampling, learning parts by ear, or rescuing an old live recording. [AudioShake](https://www.audioshake.ai/) is one of the best-known dedicated tools. Stem splitting is also now built into major DAWs like Logic Pro and FL Studio, so check what you already own before you pay for something new.

**Why you'll love it:** you can pull the bass line out of a song to learn it, or rebuild an old band recording whose multitracks are long gone.

### Chord AI

[Chord AI](https://chordai.net/) listens to a song and shows you the chords in real time. It's a fast way to learn covers, and a handy check when you're writing and want to know what you just played.

**Why you'll love it:** it saves a lot of rewinding when a bandmate sends you a song to learn before rehearsal.

## Hardware

### Native Instruments Maschine+

[Maschine+](https://www.native-instruments.com/) is a standalone groovebox: pads, sounds and sequencing with no computer needed. It's built for sketching beats on the couch and for live sets.

**Why you'll love it:** you can make music anywhere, without staring at a screen.

### ROLI Seaboard RISE 2

The [Seaboard RISE 2](https://roli.com/) replaces piano keys with a soft, continuous surface. Slide, press and wiggle to bend pitch, swell volume and shape tone as you play, much like a string player or singer would.

**Why you'll love it:** it brings expression to synth parts that a regular keyboard can't.

## Services

### LANDR

[LANDR](https://www.landr.com/) started with AI mastering and has grown into a toolkit for releasing music, with mastering, distribution, samples and plugins in one place.

**Why you'll love it:** you get a loud, polished master of a demo in minutes, before you're ready to pay a mastering engineer.

### Splice

[Splice](https://splice.com/) is a huge library of royalty-free samples and loops, with search that helps you find sounds that fit what you're working on.

**Why you'll love it:** you'll spend less time digging for the right snare and more time finishing songs.

## The bottom line

None of these tools replace good songs or good players. They do take a lot of the busywork out of making music at home, so you can spend more of your time writing and playing with other people.
