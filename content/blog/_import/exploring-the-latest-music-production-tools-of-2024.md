This year, we’re seeing an array of new tools and technologies in music production that are set to elevate your creative process. From AI-powered software to innovative hardware, these advancements are opening up exciting possibilities for musicians and producers alike. Here are the latest music production tools of 2024.

## **1. Cutting-Edge Software**

### **a. [AudioShake](http://"AudioShake" by AudioShake)**

AudioShake is revolutionizing the way producers handle stems and audio. It uses advanced AI to split recordings into individual elements like vocals, drums, and instruments. This capability is invaluable for remixing, sampling, or enhancing live recordings.

**Key Features:**

- AI-driven stem separation.
- High-quality audio extraction.
- Seamless integration with DAWs.

**Why You’ll Love It:** AudioShake simplifies remixing and sampling, allowing you to rework existing tracks effortlessly and with professional results. AudioShake’s ingenuity is why it was included in the latest music production tools of 2024.

### **b. [Chord AI](https://chordai.net/)**

Chord AI is a remarkable tool for musicians looking to understand and utilize complex chord progressions. It uses machine learning to analyze and suggest chord structures based on your melody or song progression, helping you craft more sophisticated harmonies.

**Key Features:**

- Real-time chord recognition and suggestion.
- Integration with popular DAWs.
- Customizable chord progressions.

**Why You’ll Love It:** Chord AI enhances your songwriting by providing harmonic suggestions that fit your style, making it easier to create intricate musical pieces.

## **2. Innovative Hardware**

### **a. [Native Instruments Maschine+ Mk3](https://www.sweetwater.com/store/detail/MaschinePlus--native-instruments-maschine-plus-standalone-production-and-performance-instrument)**

The latest iteration of Native Instruments’ Maschine+ brings standalone production power to the forefront. With a built-in processor, it allows you to create music without needing a computer, offering a more tactile and streamlined workflow.

**Key Features:**

- Standalone production with built-in processor.
- High-resolution color display.
- Enhanced performance pads and controls.

**Why You’ll Love It:** [Maschine+ Mk3 ](https://www.sweetwater.com/store/detail/MaschinePlus--native-instruments-maschine-plus-standalone-production-and-performance-instrument)offers the freedom to create music anywhere, with powerful features that cater to both studio and live performance needs.

### **b. [ROLI Seaboard RISE 2](https://roli.com/products/seaboard/rise2)**

[The Seaboard RISE 2 ](https://roli.com/products/seaboard/rise2)continues to push the boundaries of expressive music creation. With its unique surface that allows multidimensional control of sound, you can modulate pitch, volume, and timbre intuitively as you play, offering an unparalleled level of expressiveness.

**Key Features:**

- Continuous touch surface for multidimensional control.
- Enhanced sensitivity and tactile feedback.
- Integration with major DAWs and virtual instruments.

**Why You’ll Love It:** The Seaboard RISE 2’s innovative design provides a new level of expressiveness and control, perfect for both experimental and traditional musicians.

## **3. Revolutionary Tools**

### **a. [LANDR Studio](https://www.landr.com/)**

LANDR Studio is a cloud-based music production platform that offers AI-driven mastering, collaborative tools, and seamless integration with your workflow. It allows multiple users to work on the same project in real-time, providing a streamlined experience for remote collaboration.

**Key Features:**

- AI-driven mastering and mixing tools.
- Real-time collaboration and project sharing.
- Secure cloud storage.

**Why You’ll Love It:**[ LANDR Studio](https://www.landr.com/) makes professional-quality mastering accessible and facilitates effortless collaboration, making it an essential tool for modern music production.

This update makes it easier to find the perfect sounds for your projects, with suggestions based on your previous downloads and preferences.

**Key Features:**

- AI-curated sample collections.
- Personalized sound recommendations.

**Why You’ll Love It:** Splice Sounds 2.0 provides a more intuitive way to discover new sounds, helping you find inspiration quickly and efficiently.
